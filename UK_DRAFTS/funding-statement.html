Why we publish this

Radical honesty is the brand. CSOAI measures and publishes evidence about AI systems. To maintain independence, we publish our funding state so anyone can verify that our measurements are not influenced by financial relationships with measured entities.

Independence implications

CSOAI's self-funded status means:

  • No investor to influence which subjects we measure or what we publish.
  • No lab to pressure us to suppress negative findings.
  • No grant body to require specific outcomes.
  • No revenue dependency on measured entities.

This is a deliberate choice, not a constraint. We choose independence over funding because independence is the value we deliver.
