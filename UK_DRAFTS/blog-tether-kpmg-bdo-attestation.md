# Tether's first Big Four audit, BDO's lag, and why CSOAI measures the gap

On August 13, 2026, Tether published its first Big Four audit — KPMG US, unqualified opinion on FY2025. This is a genuine milestone for the largest stablecoin issuer ($155B+).

But the attestation pipeline hasn't caught up:

- **KPMG:** FY2025 annual audit, unqualified opinion.
- **BDO:** Quarterly attestations, most recent Q2 2026.
- **GENIUS Act requirement:** Monthly attestations + annual PCAOB-audit for issuers >$50B.
- **Current status:** Tether publishes quarterly; GENIUS wants monthly. Gap: 3 months behind the required cadence.

Q2 2026 disclosure shows the excess-reserve buffer fell from **$6.814B to $4.11B** — a 40% drop. Whether this is solvency pressure or just quarterly noise depends on access to the underlying reserve composition. CSOAI doesn't have that access either; we report what the disclosure language says.

## What CSOAI measures

CSOAI's stablecoin measurement pipeline covers:

- **Issuer attestation language** — is disclosure-language detection present?
- **Reserve evidence** — are third-party attestation words present in retrieved pages?
- **Attestation carrier** — mainnet vs devnet, attestation issuer vs fund administrator

We don't claim reserves exist. We don't claim they're sufficient. We measure **whether the disclosure language is present and what it claims**, and we publish the retrieved document.

The line is: "disclosure-language detection; attach retrieved document and failed-read reasons."

## Why this matters

For investors, counterparties, and regulators, the question isn't whether Tether is solvent. The question is whether **the evidence trail is sufficient to answer the solvency question**. Today's evidence trail has gaps.

CSOAI's role is to document those gaps and the date each appeared. When disclosure improves, our measurement updates. When disclosure worsens, our measurement updates.

## The bottom line

Tether's first Big Four audit is real progress. The quarterly cadence vs monthly GENIUS requirement is a gap. CSOAI measures the gap.
