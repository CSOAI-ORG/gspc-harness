# CSOAI Sponsorship Pitch
## 2026-09-16

CSOAI does not accept payment from measured entities. Sponsorship from foundations/labs that don't run measured AI is welcome. This is the pitch for those sponsors.

---

## THE ASK

CSOAI is looking for sponsorship to fund **public AI measurement infrastructure that no paid entity controls**.

The sponsor receives:
- Named acknowledgement on CSOAI's funding statement page
- Optional alignment on which axes or models are covered
- Quarterly impact reports (signed evidence that the sponsor's funded work ran)
- No editorial control over measurement methodology or results

The sponsor does NOT receive:
- Special treatment of measurement results
- Suppression of any model measurement
- Any compliance or conformity certification
- Any "approval" or endorsement from CSOAI

---

## WHY THIS MATTERS

Three regulatory windows are open in 2026-2027:

1. **EU AI Act high-risk enforcement** — Annex III categories (credit, insurance, biometric, employment, etc.) require evidence of AI behavior.

2. **California SB 813** — establishes Independent Verification Organizations (IVOs). Rulemaking begins 2027. Registry (AB 1405) opens January 2029.

3. **UK AISI evaluations** — UK AI Safety Institute runs evaluations using Inspect framework. CSOAI's harness is built on Inspect.

Each regime needs independent, third-party verifiable evidence of AI behavior. CSOAI's signed evidence trail is the only open infrastructure that produces it.

A sponsor underwrites this public infrastructure.

---

## WHAT THE SPONSORSHIP FUNDS

CSOAI's annual operational costs include:

- **Signing infrastructure:** HSM usage, Rekor log submission, OTS Bitcoin anchoring
- **Verification infrastructure:** Public verifier at councilof.ai/gspc-verify
- **Continuous measurement:** Running GSPC harness against new open-source models
- **Corrections feed:** Machine-derived log of measurement corrections
- **API infrastructure:** MCP server, A2A agent card, x402 catalog, live board API

A typical sponsorship level covers one of:

| Level | Annual USD | What it funds |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| **Runway** | $5,000 | 90 days of continuous measurements for top open-source models |
| **Coverage** | $25,000 | 1 axis covered continuously for 12 months across all open-source models |
| **Inference** | $50,000 | 6 months of fresh measurements on demand + corrections feed |
| **Infrastructure** | $100,000+ | 12 months of CSOAI's entire public surface (sponsor named) |

Larger sponsorships are negotiable. All sponsorships are documented publicly.

## WHY STRUCTURAL INDEPENDENCE MATTERS

If CSOAI took payment from measured entities:
- Conflicts of interest emerge (buyer wants favorable result)
- Trust erodes (measurement becomes marketing)
- The "measurement, not certification" position becomes hollow

CSOAI's no-payment-from-measured-entities policy is the only structural protection. The sponsor underwrites this independence.

A buyer using CSOAI's signed evidence in their EU AI Act documentation can cite:
- The verification keys
- The methodology
- The corrections feed
- The fact that the measurer was not paid by the measured entity

That last point is structural, not contractual. The sponsor's investment is what makes it possible.

---

## WHAT THIS IS NOT

This is NOT:
- A "buy an endorsement" opportunity
- A "sponsor = certified" arrangement
- A way to suppress or modify CSOAI's measurement of any AI

This IS:
- Funding for public infrastructure
- A way to expand coverage (more axes, more models, more frequent updates)
- A way to ensure CSOAI's structural independence holds

## FAQ

**Q: If we sponsor, can we tell people we sponsor?**
A: Yes. Sponsor acknowledgement is at https://councilof.ai/funding-statement.html.

**Q: Can we request specific models be measured?**
A: You can suggest. CSOAI decides what to measure based on methodology and resources.

**Q: Can we suppress models from being measured?**
A: No. CSOAI's no-payment-from-measured-entities policy applies equally to all entities.

**Q: Can we have our logo on the public surface?**
A: Yes, on the funding statement page. Not on the verifier or board (those are content-addressed and signed; adding a logo would change the hash).

**Q: Is sponsorship tax-deductible?**
A: CSOAI Ltd is a UK company. Sponsorship is a commercial transaction, not a donation. Tax treatment depends on sponsor's jurisdiction.

**Q: Can we get a multi-year commitment?**
A: Yes, with documentation. Multi-year sponsorships can lock in coverage scope.

**Q: What if we disagree with a measurement?**
A: Sponsor can submit a correction request. CSOAI evaluates via the same correction process as any other entity.

**Q: What if we want to exit?**
A: Sponsors can step away at any renewal cycle. No penalty. Funding statement updated.
