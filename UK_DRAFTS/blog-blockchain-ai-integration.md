# Why CSOAI matters for Layer 0/1 blockchain AI integration

Layer 0/1 blockchains increasingly host AI components. CSOAI measures those AI components with the same signed evidence infrastructure used in finance.

## The Layer 0/1 + AI convergence

Blockchains are increasingly integrating AI:

- **NEAR Protocol** — AI primitives built into the L1
- **Internet Computer (ICP)** — AI canisters (smart contracts running ML)
- **Solana** — high throughput for AI inference
- **Arc (Circle's L1)** — launching Sep 16 with AI/agent focus
- **Akash Network** — decentralized GPU marketplace
- **Render Network** — distributed GPU rendering for AI
- **io.net** — distributed compute for AI
- **Bittensor (TAO)** — decentralized AI network
- **Fetch.ai** — AI agents on-chain
- **SingularityNET** — AI marketplace
- **Ocean Protocol** — data marketplace for AI
- **Numerai** — crowdsourced hedge fund AI

Each uses AI in different ways. CSOAI measures the AI components.

## What CSOAI provides for L0/L1 + AI

CSOAI provides signed evidence that:

1. **The AI components work as claimed.** CSOAI's 22-axis harness measures specific behaviors.
2. **The AI components don't introduce new risks.** CSOAI's honesty register documents uncertainties.
3. **The AI components are auditable.** CSOAI's methodology is in source code.

A blockchain project can cite CSOAI's signed cards in their governance documentation.

## Why AI on L0/L1 needs CSOAI

Running AI on a blockchain doesn't make the AI correct. The AI components are still:

- Subject to bias
- Subject to error
- Subject to vendor claims
- Subject to Goodhart's Law

CSOAI's measurement applies the same standards whether the AI runs:

- On AWS
- On a private cloud
- On a blockchain node
- On a decentralized GPU network

The AI behavior is what matters. CSOAI measures AI behavior.

## What CSOAI doesn't do for L0/L1

CSOAI doesn't measure:

- Blockchain consensus correctness
- Smart contract security
- Token economics
- Network decentralization

These are L0/L1-specific measurements. CSOAI focuses on the AI components.

## Why CSOAI's structural independence matters for decentralized AI

Decentralized AI projects have a credibility problem:

- The project itself stakes its own tokens
- Validators may have financial interest in certain outcomes
- The team's claims are unverifiable

CSOAI's structural independence (no payment from measured entities) solves this:

- CSOAI doesn't stake tokens
- CSOAI doesn't validate
- CSOAI's measurement is independent

A decentralized AI project's CSOAI measurement is more credible than the project's self-measurement.

## What this looks like for NEAR, ICP, Bittensor

For each:

- **NEAR:** CSOAI measures the AI primitives (NXM, NAI) on chain
- **ICP:** CSOAI measures the AI canisters running on ICP
- **Bittensor:** CSOAI measures the subnet validators' AI outputs
- **Akash/ Render / io.net:** CSOAI measures the AI workloads running on the GPU network

Each gets signed evidence for their AI components.
