CSOAI Ltd (UK, Companies House 16939677) is a UK AI measurement body that has built a machine-readable, cryptographically signed AI governance framework. We wish to submit a Pillar II voluntary pledge demonstrating our commitment to transparent, verifiable AI governance.

## Our Pledge

CSOAI Ltd commits to:

1. **Publish machine-readable AI governance evidence** for all measured AI systems, with signed cards and content-addressed records
2. **Maintain a publicly auditable corrections ledger** at https://councilof.ai/feeds/corrections.xml
3. **Provide independent verification tools** at https://councilof.ai/gspc-verify/ that allow any third party to verify our evidence without trusting our servers
4. **Use UK AISI's Inspect framework** as our primary evaluation engine (gspc-harness, https://github.com/CSOAI-ORG/gspc-harness)
5. **Publish signed receipts** (inspect-signed-receipt on PyPI) for all evaluation runs

## Our Evidence

- 22-axis GSPC framework (14 behavioural + 8 deterministic financial axes)
- 1,119 measured cells with signed cards (371 signed, 257 in public-root Merkle tree)
- Live public verifier at https://councilof.ai/gspc-verify/
- Machine-derived corrections ledger at https://councilof.ai/feeds/corrections.xml
- inspect-signed-receipt on PyPI (https://pypi.org/project/inspect-signed-receipt/)

## Our Independence

CSOAI Ltd maintains strict independence:
- No financial ties to measured entities
- No publication gags or pre-publication vetoes
- All evidence is publicly verifiable
- Corrections are published in a machine-derived ledger
