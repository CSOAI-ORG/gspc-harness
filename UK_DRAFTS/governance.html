Governance & Independence

CSOAI measures and publishes evidence. It does not certify, endorse, or claim regulator approval.

This page documents our governance structure, independence policy, and conflict-of-interest controls.

2. Independence policy

CSOAI maintains strict independence from the entities it measures:

  • No payment from measured entities. We do not accept payment, sponsorship, or funding from any entity whose AI systems we measure. This is a stricter standard than California SB 813 §8898.1(c)(2)(C), which permits reasonable market-rate assessed-party payments.
  • No publication gag. We do not accept pre-publication vetoes, editorial control, or publication delays from measured entities. Results publish, period.
  • Access scope disclosed on the card. If a measured entity provides access to internal systems, the scope and method are documented on the measurement card.
  • Corrections ledger governs disputes. Disputes about measurement accuracy are resolved through our publicly auditable corrections ledger (corrections.xml), not through private negotiation with the measured entity.

3. Conflict-of-interest register

As of September 2026, CSOAI has no conflicts of interest to disclose:

  • No financial ties to measured entities. CSOAI has no equity, debt, consulting, or advisory relationships with any entity whose AI systems we measure.
  • No lab funding. CSOAI has not received funding from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, or any other AI lab.
  • No regulatory affiliation. CSOAI is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of any regulatory body (AISI, FCA, SEC, NIST, or any other).
  • Self-funded. CSOAI is 100% self-funded by its sole director. External revenue to date: $0.02 via x402 protocol.

4. Key-custody statement

CSOAI uses Ed25519 signing keys for measurement receipts:

  • Signing key custody: The Ed25519 signing key is held by the sole director in a hardware security module (HSM). The key is not stored on any server, cloud service, or shared infrastructure.
  • Verification key: The public verification key is published at csoai.org/.well-known/did.json (did:web:csoai.org).
  • Key rotation: If the signing key is compromised, a new key pair will be generated and the did:web document will be updated. All previously signed receipts remain verifiable against the original public key.
  • No key sharing: The signing key is not shared with any third party, including measured entities, partners, or regulators.

5. Decision rights

  • Measurement decisions: The sole director decides which subjects to measure, which axes to apply, and when to publish results.
  • Corrections: Errors in published measurements are corrected through the corrections ledger. Corrections are published publicly and linked from the original measurement card.
  • Scope changes: Changes to the measurement methodology are documented in the versioned methodology PDF (when published) and announced on the corrections feed.

6. Key-person continuity

CSOAI is currently a sole-founder operation. In the event of key-person incapacity:

  • Open-source code: All measurement code is open source (Apache-2.0) and can be forked and maintained by any third party.
  • Published methodology: The measurement methodology is documented and reproducible. Any qualified party can replicate the measurements.
  • Verifiable receipts: All published receipts are content-addressed and independently verifiable. They do not depend on CSOAI's infrastructure to remain valid.
  • Designation request: In the event of key-person incapacity, CSOAI will request designation as a public-interest entity under California SB 813 to ensure continuity of the measurement infrastructure.
