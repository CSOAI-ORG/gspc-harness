# RWA.xyz vs CSOAI — different layers of the same tokenized-asset stack

RWA.xyz is the largest tokenized-asset analytics platform. CSOAI is independent measurement infrastructure. We're complementary, not competitive. Here's how the layers fit together.

## What RWA.xyz does

RWA.xyz provides:

- **Asset directory:** A catalogue of tokenized real-world assets
- **TVL analytics:** Total value locked by chain, issuer, asset class
- **Holder/transfer data:** Distribution analysis, transfer history
- **News and research:** Tokenized-asset market intelligence

When a buyer's question is "how big is the RWA market?", RWA.xyz answers it.

## What CSOAI does

CSOAI provides:

- **Per-asset identity verification:** Issuer + contract + chain + decimals
- **Independent attestation evidence:** Disclosure language, retrieved documents, signed receipts
- **Public verification:** Anyone can re-run CSOAI's measurements

When a buyer's question is "is this RWA's reserve claim independently verified?", CSOAI answers it.

## How they fit together

The combined stack:

1. **RWA.xyz lists the asset** with TVL, holder data, market intelligence.
2. **CSOAI measures the asset's specific claims** — reserve attestation language, disclosure scope, jurisdiction status.
3. **The signed CSOAI card** binds the issuer's claims to a specific measurement run.
4. **The public verifier** lets anyone check the card against the published keys.

RWA.xyz answers "what is the market doing?" CSOAI answers "what does the issuer actually say, and is it independently verified?"

## The overlap

Both CSOAI and RWA.xyz track tokenized assets. Both report supply and market data. The differences:

- **RWA.xyz is an analytics platform.** They aggregate public data and present it.
- **CSOAI is independent measurement.** We don't aggregate; we measure specific claims with specific evidence.
- **RWA.xyz is commercial.** They sell subscriptions and analytics products.
- **CSOAI is evidence infrastructure.** We sell x402-metered tools and public verification.

When RWA.xyz lists "Tether USDT has $155B supply," CSOAI's measurement is: "Tether's August 13, 2026 KPMG audit reports unqualified opinion on FY2025; Q2 2026 attestation buffer fell from $6.814B to $4.11B; disclosure language is present but the cadence is quarterly vs GENIUS's monthly requirement."

The first is market data. The second is independent verification of the issuer's claims.

## Why both matter

A buyer using only RWA.xyz knows the market. A buyer using only CSOAI knows the issuer's claims. A buyer using both knows both — and can compare.

That's the value of independent measurement: it doesn't replace market analytics; it adds a layer of verification.

## The C07 reference

CSOAI's UX audit explicitly recommends RWA.xyz as a reference for identity-first analytics:

> **C07 — RWA.xyz:** "Asset/domain documentation as a reference for identity-first analytics. Apply: Validate an identity-focused product schema against real instrument representations before UI comparison. Do not copy/infer: Claiming its authenticated product or every analytics view was tested here."

CSOAI uses RWA.xyz as a reference for asset identity schemas, not as a competitor.

## The bottom line

RWA.xyz + CSOAI = market data + independent verification. Different layers, same goal.
