# Why CSOAI's pricing page explains what's free and what's paid

CSOAI's pricing page at `UK_DRAFTS/pricing.html` explicitly distinguishes free evidence from paid services. The honesty principle: never hide costs.

## What's free

CSOAI's free tier includes:

- **Public board data:** All current measurements, accessible via API and the stablecoin browser
- **Public root tree:** Every signed card's Merkle inclusion proof
- **Public verifier:** Browser-based verification of any signed card
- **Corrections feed:** Machine-derived log of every error CSOAI finds in its own measurements
- **Methodology:** Full source code, scoring rules, item sets
- **Discussion:** GitHub issues, discussions, and PRs

The free tier is the **default**. Anyone can use CSOAI's evidence without paying anything.

## What's paid

CSOAI's paid services include:

- **x402-metered tools:** Fresh measurements on demand ($0.01 per proof)
- **Enterprise evidence assembly:** Curated evidence packages for buyers who need them
- **Sponsorship:** Foundations and labs can sponsor CSOAI's public infrastructure

The paid services are **convenience and curation**, not gatekeeping.

## Why explicit pricing

When CSOAI decided to charge for some services, we chose to:

1. **List prices publicly.** No "contact for pricing" gates.
2. **Explain what you get for each price.** No opaque packages.
3. **Explain what you DON'T get.** No hidden tier restrictions.
4. **Explain the underlying x402 mechanism.** Buyers can verify the settlement themselves on-chain.

A buyer can decide whether to pay based on the actual cost and the actual value.

## Why "free evidence" matters

When evidence is free:

1. **Buyers can verify without spending.** A journalist can verify a card without an enterprise contract.
2. **Regulators can verify without paying.** A regulator can use CSOAI's evidence without a procurement process.
3. **Competitors can verify.** Other safety vendors can use CSOAI's evidence to challenge their own measurements.
4. **The market can audit.** Anyone can check whether CSOAI's measurements are honest.

Free evidence makes the market work. Paid evidence alone creates a gatekeeper.

## Why "paid convenience" matters

When convenience is paid:

1. **Buyers can get fresh measurements without running their own infrastructure.** Not everyone has HSM access.
2. **Enterprises can get curated evidence packages.** Some buyers need hand-holding.
3. **Sponsors can fund the public infrastructure.** Foundations want to underwrite the public good.

Paid convenience is honest because:
- The underlying evidence is still public
- The paid service adds value beyond the public evidence
- The buyer knows exactly what they're paying for

## The honesty principle

CSOAI's honesty principle:

> **Never hide costs.**

This means:

- Public evidence is free; that's not hidden
- Paid services have public prices; that's not hidden
- The relationship between free and paid is documented; that's not hidden
- The pricing mechanism (x402) is open-source; that's not hidden

A buyer can know everything before spending anything.

## What CSOAI is NOT

CSOAI is not:

- A pricing-tier vendor
- A "safety score" SaaS
- A gatekeeper to AI evidence
- A vendor with proprietary scoring

CSOAI is evidence infrastructure with optional convenience services.

## The bottom line

Free evidence, paid convenience, public prices, hidden nothing. That's CSOAI's honest pricing.
