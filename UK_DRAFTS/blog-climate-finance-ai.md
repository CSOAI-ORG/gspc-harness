# Why CSOAI matters for climate finance AI — carbon credits, green bonds, ESG

Climate finance increasingly uses AI for carbon credit verification, green bond assessment, ESG scoring. CSOAI's signed evidence layer provides verifiable measurement for these AI components.

## The climate finance AI landscape

Climate finance uses AI for:

- **Carbon credit verification** — MRV (measurement, reporting, verification) of carbon offsets
- **Green bond assessment** — verifying use-of-proceeds for green bonds
- **ESG scoring** — measuring companies' environmental, social, governance
- **Climate risk modeling** — physical and transition risk for portfolios
- **Carbon accounting** — Scope 1, 2, 3 emissions tracking
- **Sustainable investing** — screening funds for sustainability claims

Each uses AI. CSOAI measures the AI components.

## What CSOAI provides for climate finance AI

CSOAI provides signed evidence for:

1. **Carbon credit AI:** CSOAI measures whether AI correctly verifies carbon offsets.
2. **Green bond AI:** CSOAI measures whether AI correctly classifies use-of-proceeds.
3. **ESG AI:** CSOAI measures whether AI correctly scores ESG criteria.
4. **Climate risk AI:** CSOAI measures whether AI correctly assesses climate risk.
5. **Carbon accounting AI:** CSOAI measures whether AI correctly tracks emissions.

Each measurement produces a signed card.

## Why this matters now

Several climate finance regulatory windows are open:

- **EU CSRD** (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive) — requiring ESG disclosures
- **EU Taxonomy** — defining what's "green"
- **SFDR** (Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation) — fund disclosures
- **SEC Climate Disclosure** — US public company climate disclosures
- **ISSB IFRS S1/S2** — global sustainability disclosure standards

Each requires evidence of AI behavior for any AI used in climate finance. CSOAI provides that evidence.

## The greenwashing problem

Greenwashing is the biggest risk in climate finance:

- A "green" bond that funds fossil fuel projects
- Carbon credits that don't represent real reductions
- ESG scores that misrepresent company behavior
- AI models that systematically overstate sustainability

CSOAI's signed evidence helps:

- Verify the AI's methodology for ESG scoring
- Document the AI's behavior on specific test conditions
- Surface methodological errors via corrections feed
- Provide independent third-party verification

## What CSOAI doesn't do

CSOAI doesn't measure:

- Carbon offsets themselves (that's a different measurement)
- ESG criteria themselves (those are policy decisions)
- Sustainability claims themselves (those are human judgments)
- The actual sustainability of a company/project

CSOAI measures the AI components that make climate finance decisions. The AI is a tool; the decision is human.

## What this looks like for ESG platforms

An ESG scoring platform:

1. Uses AI to score companies on ESG criteria
2. Publishes scores to investors
3. Investors make decisions based on scores

CSOAI's evidence layer:

1. Measures the AI's behavior on benchmark ESG scenarios
2. Documents the methodology used
3. Surfaces corrections when methodology changes
4. Provides independent verification

Investors who cite CSOAI's cards can defend their reliance on ESG scores.

## What this looks like for carbon credits

A carbon credit verifier:

1. Uses AI to verify carbon offset claims
2. Issues carbon credits based on AI's output
3. Buyers purchase credits to offset emissions

CSOAI's evidence layer:

1. Measures the AI's behavior on benchmark offset scenarios
2. Documents the methodology used
3. Surfaces corrections
4. Provides independent verification

Buyers who cite CSOAI's cards can defend their offset purchases.

## What CSOAI is NOT

CSOAI is NOT:

- A carbon credit registry
- An ESG rating agency
- A sustainability certification body
- A green bond verifier

CSOAI is measurement infrastructure for the AI components of climate finance systems.

Climate finance needs verifiable AI. CSOAI's signed evidence layer provides the measurement infrastructure for CSRD, EU Taxonomy, SFDR, SEC Climate, ISSB IFRS S1/S2 compliance.
