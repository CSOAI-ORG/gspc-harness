1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your experience.

2. What cookies do we use?

We use no tracking cookies.

Our websites are designed to work without cookies. Specifically:

  • No analytics cookies: We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any cookie-based tracking.
  • No advertising cookies: We do not serve advertisements or track you across websites.
  • No session cookies: Our free tools do not require login, so no session cookies are set.
  • No consent banner: Since we use no cookies, we do not need a cookie consent banner.

3. Third-party cookies

Some third-party services we use may set their own cookies:

  • Stripe: Payment processing may use cookies for fraud prevention (Stripe cookie policy).
  • GitHub: If you interact with our GitHub issues, GitHub's cookie policy applies (GitHub privacy policy).
  • Cloudflare: CDN security may use cookies for bot detection (Cloudflare privacy policy).

4. How to control cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Firefox
  • Safari
  • Edge
