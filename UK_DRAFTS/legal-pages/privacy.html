1. What we collect

We collect the minimum data necessary to operate our services:

  • Payment data: When you make a purchase via Stripe, we receive a transaction ID, amount, currency, and timestamp. We do not store card numbers — Stripe handles that.
  • API usage data: When you call our MCP tools or x402 endpoints, we log the tool name, timestamp, IP address, and response status. We do not log request/response payloads.
  • Website analytics: We use privacy-respecting analytics (no cookies, no fingerprinting). Page views are counted, not tracked.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • No account registration is required to use our free tools.
  • No cookies are set for tracking purposes.
  • No personal data is sold, shared, or rented to third parties.
  • No behavioral profiling or advertising tracking.
