# CSOAI's roadmap to becoming a California IVO

California's SB 813 establishes Independent Verification Organizations (IVOs). The rulemaking is scheduled for 2027. CSOAI's existing infrastructure maps to every IVO requirement. Here's the roadmap to formal designation.

## What SB 813 requires

SB 813 §8898.1(c)(2)(C) addresses IVO independence. It permits reasonable market-rate assessed-party payments that are not conditioned on assessment results.

AB 1405 establishes an AI Auditor Registry by 2029. Registration will be mandatory for covered audits from that date.

CSOAI's existing infrastructure maps directly:

CSOAI's existing stack exceeds what most regulated markets require.

## Risks and mitigation

1. **Rulemaking could narrow IVO requirements.** CSOAI comments proactively to keep requirements broad enough for technical verification.
2. **Registration fees could be prohibitive.** CSOAI's funding statement covers self-funded status; the goal is to subsidise registration if fees are high.
3. **Geographic requirement could exclude UK companies.** SB 813 doesn't currently require California presence; CSOAI's UK incorporation doesn't appear to be a blocker.
4. **Interference with measured entities.** CSOAI's no-paid-entities policy is structural protection.
