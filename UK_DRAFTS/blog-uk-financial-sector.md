# Why CSOAI's evidence infrastructure matters for the UK financial sector

## The UK regulatory landscape

UK financial services operate under multiple regimes simultaneously:

1. **UK AISI** (UK AI Safety Institute) — voluntary safety evaluations
2. **FCA** (Financial Conduct Authority) — AI guidance, Senior Managers Regime, Consumer Duty
3. **PRA** (Prudential Regulation Authority) — for insurers and banks, SS1/23 on AI/ML
4. **Bank of England** — for systemic risk, financial stability
5. **PSR** (Payment Systems Regulator) — for payment services
6. **ICO** (Information Commissioner's Office) — UK GDPR, data protection
7. **EU AI Act** — for UK firms serving EU customers
8. **HM Treasury** — AI in financial services policy

Each framework requires evidence of AI governance. None provides the evidence infrastructure. CSOAI does.
