# Why CSOAI's evidence infrastructure matters for the UK financial sector

UK banks, insurance, and financial services face multiple overlapping regulatory requirements: EU AI Act, UK AISI, and FCA. CSOAI's signed evidence infrastructure is the measurement layer they need.

## The regulatory landscape

UK financial services operate under:

1. **EU AI Act** (where applicable to UK firms serving EU customers)
2. **UK AISI** (voluntary safety evaluations)
3. **FCA AI Framework** (existing + new)
4. **PRA** (for insurers)
5. **Bank of England** (for systemic risk)
6. **PSR** (for payment services)

Each framework requires evidence of AI governance. None provides the evidence infrastructure. CSOAI does.

## What CSOAI provides for UK financial AI

CSOAI's signed evidence layer provides:

1. **Risk-tier classification** (EU AI Act Annex III): CSOAI's AX-01 governance axis classifies models
2. **Safety calibration** (high-risk AI): CSOAI's AX-02 safety axis measures calibration
3. **Transparency disclosure** (Article 50): CSOAI's signed cards document transparency
4. **Quality management** (Article 17): CSOAI's methodology-as-code provides quality documentation
5. **Post-market monitoring** (Article 72): CSOAI's corrections feed provides monitoring capability

Each piece is signed, content-addressed, and verifiable.

## What UK firms specifically need

UK financial firms using AI need:

1. **Evidence of compliance with risk-tier classification.** CSOAI provides it.
2. **Documentation of testing methodology.** CSOAI's methodology is in source code.
3. **Public verification of measurements.** CSOAI's verifier runs in any browser.
4. **Corrections capability.** CSOAI's corrections feed handles errors publicly.
5. **Independence from vendors.** CSOAI never accepts payment from measured entities.

These are exactly what UK financial AI governance needs.
