# Why CSOAI doesn't compete with Anthropic, OpenAI, or any model developer

CSOAI measures. Anthropic and OpenAI build models. Different business, different revenue, different customers. The structural independence is the trust anchor.

## What model developers do

Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Meta, DeepSeek, Mistral — they all:

- Build, train, fine-tune, and deploy AI models
- Sell access to those models (API, subscription, enterprise)
- Compete on capability, price, safety claims, brand
- Make money when customers use their models

Their revenue is "model usage." More usage = more revenue.

## What CSOAI does

CSOAI:

- Measures model behavior on specific test conditions
- Produces signed, content-addressed receipts
- Publishes evidence anyone can verify
- Charges for fresh measurements (x402) and enterprise evidence assembly

CSOAI's revenue is "evidence production." More independent measurements = more trust.

## Why this isn't competition

Model developers want their models to:

- Be used more
- Have higher scores on common benchmarks
- Have favorable public narratives about safety

CSOAI measures models on:

- Specific test conditions
- Specific methodologies
- Without accepting payment from measured entities

CSOAI's measurement doesn't depend on which model "wins." A measurement of GPT-4o and a measurement of Claude 3.5 Sonnet produce comparable signed receipts. The buyer picks. CSOAI's revenue scales with measurement activity, not with which model wins.

## Why this benefits model developers

Counterintuitively, model developers benefit from CSOAI's existence:

1. **Independent verification supports their claims.** A model developer saying "we're safe" without evidence is unverifiable. With CSOAI's signed receipts, the same claim becomes falsifiable.
2. **Independent comparison supports the market.** Buyers comparing CSOAI receipts can pick the right model. They don't need to trust the developer.
3. **Independent corrections support the developers.** When CSOAI publishes a correction, it's a specific error with a specific fix. The developer can incorporate it.

A model developer who wants CSOAI's receipts to exist is a model developer who benefits from independent measurement.

## Why this benefits CSOAI

CSOAI benefits from model developer adoption:

1. **More models to measure** = more evidence = more authority
2. **More buyers asking for evidence** = more usage = more revenue
3. **More regulators requiring evidence** = more demand

The model developer market is CSOAI's downstream demand, not its competitor.

## The structural independence

CSOAI's no-paid-entities policy is the key structural protection:

- CSOAI cannot be bribed by a model developer to give them a favorable measurement
- CSOAI cannot be pressured by a model developer to suppress an unfavorable measurement
- The signed receipt stands on its own; the developer's wallet is irrelevant

This is the same protection a financial auditor needs. The auditor measures the company without taking payment from the company.

## Why CSOAI can be a critic AND a customer

CSOAI can measure GPT-4o AND have customers who use GPT-4o. CSOAI can publish a finding that Anthropic's safety claim is weak AND have customers who use Claude.

The measurement is independent of CSOAI's commercial relationships. The customer relationship is independent of CSOAI's measurements.

This is unusual in AI — most safety vendors are tied to specific models or labs. CSOAI is **model-agnostic** by structure.

## The bottom line

CSOAI measures; model developers build. Independence is structural. The model developer market is CSOAI's downstream demand.
