# Why CSOAI's UK Companies House registration matters for evidence infrastructure

CSOAI is a UK company (Companies House 16939677). That's not branding — it's structural. UK law provides CSOAI's accountability framework.

## What UK Companies House is

Companies House is the UK's registrar of companies. Every UK-registered company:

- Has a unique company number (CSOAI's is 16939677)
- Files annual confirmation statements
- Files annual accounts (depending on size)
- Has publicly searchable records
- Is subject to UK company law

CSOAI Ltd is a private limited company registered in England and Wales.

## What this provides

UK Companies House registration provides:

1. **Legal personality.** CSOAI can enter contracts, hold assets, and be sued in its own name.
2. **Public records.** Anyone can look up CSOAI's filings at `find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk`.
3. **Statutory obligations.** Annual filings, director disclosures, financial accounts.
4. **Court jurisdiction.** Disputes against CSOAI are resolved in UK courts.
5. **Director accountability.** Directors have legal duties under the Companies Act 2006.

## Why this matters for evidence infrastructure

A measurement framework needs accountability. If CSOAI produces a false measurement:

- A buyer can sue CSOAI in UK courts
- A regulator can investigate CSOAI under UK law
- The Companies House record shows CSOAI's filings
- The director's duties under the Companies Act create personal liability

Without incorporation, accountability would be limited to reputation. With incorporation, accountability is structural.

## What the public record shows

Anyone can look up CSOAI Ltd on Companies House and see:

- Company number 16939677
- Registered office address
- Director(s)
- Filing history
- Annual confirmation statements
- SIC codes (Standard Industrial Classification)

The filing history is a public audit trail of CSOAI's corporate compliance.

## Why UK rather than US

CSOAI chose UK incorporation because:

1. **Founder is UK-based.** Nicholas Templeman is in the UK.
2. **UK law is well-developed for tech companies.** Common law traditions work for software/IP.
3. **UK has strong transparency rules.** Companies House records are publicly searchable.
4. **UK is a major AI jurisdiction.** UK AISI is the leading AI safety institute.
5. **UK Companies House is accessible globally.** Anyone can look up CSOAI from anywhere.

The choice isn't about avoiding US regulation. CSOAI is subject to UK regulation (UK GDPR, UK AI regulations as they emerge) just as a US company would be subject to US regulation.

## What CSOAI is NOT

CSOAI is NOT:

- A US LLC or C-corp (that's a different structure)
- A shell company (CSOAI has filed annual accounts and confirmation statements)
- An offshore entity (CSOAI is registered in England and Wales)
- A government agency (CSOAI is a private limited company)

CSOAI is a UK SME with all the standard obligations and protections of UK company law.

## What this means for evidence buyers

A buyer using CSOAI's evidence can:

1. Look up CSOAI Ltd on Companies House
2. See who the directors are
3. Check the filing history for compliance
4. Sue CSOAI in UK courts if needed
5. Rely on UK consumer protection law

The buyer's recourse is structural, not just reputational.

## The bottom line

UK Companies House registration provides CSOAI's accountability framework. It's structural, not branding.
