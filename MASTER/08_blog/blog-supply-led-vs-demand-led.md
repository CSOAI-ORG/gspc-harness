# Why CSOAI is supply-led, not demand-led — and why that matters

CSOAI ships public measurement infrastructure first. Customers, revenue, and adoption follow. This is the opposite of most AI startups and the only honest approach to AI governance.

## The two startup models

**Demand-led startups:**
1. Identify a customer pain point
2. Sell to customers
3. Build the product
4. Hope the product matches the sale

**Supply-led startups:**
1. Build the product
2. Ship publicly
3. Customers discover
4. Adopt and pay over time

Demand-led is faster to revenue. Supply-led is slower but creates **structural advantages**.

## CSOAI is supply-led

CSOAI's approach:

1. Built the GSPC harness — open-source, Apache-2.0
2. Shipped `inspect-signed-receipt` to PyPI
3. Published the public Merkle root tree, Rekor witness, OTS/Bitcoin anchor
4. Built the public verifier at `councilof.ai/gspc-verify`
5. Published the corrections feed at `councilof.ai/feeds/corrections.xml`
6. Wrote the governance page, funding statement, how-to-cite
7. Launched the MCP server, A2A agent card, x402 integration
8. Submitted to the GitHub MCP Registry

Revenue and customers follow from this infrastructure. Not the other way around.

## Why supply-led is the right model here

### Trust requires independent infrastructure

A buyer doesn't trust an organization that built infrastructure to suit a customer's needs — that's what vendors do. A buyer trusts an organization that built infrastructure independently, then opened it for buyers to verify and adopt.

### Regulation defines infrastructure before adoption

California SB 813, EU AI Act, FDIC PS-01, NIST AI 300-1 — all of these will require infrastructure that doesn't exist yet. CSOAI built it first, ahead of the regulatory demand.

### Honesty is operationalized before commercialization

The corrections ledger, honesty register, signing key custody, funding policy — these are operational properties of CSOAI's infrastructure. They can't be retrofitted after commercialization. They have to exist before the first paying customer.

### Adoption follows public infrastructure

Every AI lab uses Inspect. Every agent uses MCP. Every A2A agent reads `agent-card.json`. Every x402 client pays on Base. CSOAI's infrastructure plugs into these universal standards, so adoption follows adoption of the standards — which is happening now, not when CSOAI finds a sales team.

## The cost of supply-led

Supply-led is slow. CSOAI has been running for 2+ years. External revenue is $0.02.

But the alternative — demand-led AI governance — doesn't work. The buyer doesn't know what they need until the infrastructure exists. If we waited for buyers to ask for signed AI evidence, we'd be building it in 2027 when SB 813 IVOs are finally defined.

## The bottom line

Build first. Show it. Open it. Wait for the market to catch up. That's supply-led AI governance.
