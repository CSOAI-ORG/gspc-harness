## EU AI Act obligations for robo-advisors

Robo-advisors face EU AI Act obligations depending on use case:

- **Article 50:** Disclosure that user interacts with AI (transparency)
- **Annex III 5(b):** Creditworthiness evaluation (for any lending components)
- **Annex III 5(c):** Insurance risk assessment (for any insurance components)
- **Annex III 7:** Migration and asylum (N/A for most robo-advisors)

CSOAI's evidence layer addresses each applicable obligation.

## What this means for fiduciary duty

Robo-advisors have a fiduciary duty to clients. The fiduciary duty includes:

- Acting in client's best interest
- Disclosing conflicts of interest
- Providing suitable advice
- Documenting decisions

CSOAI's signed evidence supports fiduciary documentation:

- The AI was tested for suitability
- The AI's behavior is documented
- The methodology is auditable
- The corrections are tracked

A fiduciary using CSOAI's evidence has stronger documentation than one relying on self-reports.
